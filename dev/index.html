SpikingNeuralNetworks.jl

Simulate networks of spiking neurons in Julia: point and dendritic neuron models, conductance and receptor synapses, delays, long- and short-term plasticity, Poisson and spike-time inputs, and recording, analysis and plotting of the results.

using SpikingNeuralNetworks
SNN.@load_units
E = SNN.IF(N = 100)
EE = SNN.SpikingSynapse(E, E, :ge; conn = (p = 0.1, μ = 2nS))
drive = SNN.Stimulus(SNN.PoissonFixed(rate = 2kHz), E, :ge)
model = SNN.compose(; E, EE, drive, silent = true)
SNN.monitor!(E, [:fire])
SNN.sim!(model, 1s)
SNN.firing_rate(E; interval = 0:10ms:1s, pop_average = true)

Packages

SpikingNeuralNetworks loads and re-exports three packages of the JuliaSNN organisation, and defines the alias SNN:

  • SNNModels: models and simulation engine;
  • SNNPlots: plots of the recordings (Makie);
  • SNNUtils: stimulation protocols and analysis.
