Changelog

All history of the library, newest release first. The rest of the documentation describes the current state only. Under each release, Fixed lists the changes that alter simulation or analysis results, Improved all other changes. Complete per-package changelogs: CHANGELOG.md of SNNModels, SNNUtils, SNNPlots and CalciumSpike, and CHANGELOG_2.0.0.md of SNNModels (every 2.0.0 change with its migration).

2.1.0 (SpikingNeuralNetworks 2.1.0; SNNModels 2.1.0 to 2.3.0; SNNUtils 1.1.0; CalciumSpike 1.1.0)

Fixed

  • Receptor kinetics (SNNModels 2.2.0). ReceptorSynapse and MultiReceptorSynapse (soma and dendrites of Tripod and BallAndStick, point neurons with receptors) peak at g0 at any dt. Before, the peak was c(dt) g0 with c = α dt / (1 - exp(-α dt)), α = 1/τr - 1/τd (AMPA and GABAa 17 to 23 % too high at dt 0.1 to 0.125 ms, NMDA 5 to 6 %, GABAb below 0.2 %). To reproduce an old run: g0_new = c(dt) g0_old, per receptor, at the dt of the old run. exact = false keeps the old discrete update (used by TripodPaper).
  • Tripod and BallAndStick soma clamp (SNNModels 2.1.0). During the back-propagating spike (up) and the refractory period (τabs) the dendrites only relaxed axially towards the clamped soma, without leak or synaptic current (NMDA included), so one somatic spike could end an NMDA plateau. They now integrate the full dendritic equation with the Heun scheme (plateau of paper Fig. 8 protocol 28.7 ms to 54.2 ms; network rate +1.5 to +2 % for Tripod, below 0.3 % for BallAndStick).
  • GABA and AMPA defaults of Tripod and BallAndStick (SNNModels 2.1.0). Somatic and dendritic GABAa E_rev -70 to -75 mV, somatic GABAa τr 0.1 to 0.3 ms, somatic AMPA τr 0.26 to 0.25 ms (values of the published Tripod code).
  • PostSpike defaults (SNNModels 2.1.0). τabs 1 to 2 ms, τA 10 to 30 ms, AP_membrane 10 to 20 mV, and Vspike of Tripod and BallAndStick -10 to 0 mV. The refractory period of IF and AdEx doubles (default IF benchmark: 315 to 238 Hz).
  • Length presets (SNNModels 2.1.0). proximal_distal and proximal_proximal are the fixed lengths (150/400 um, 150/150 um) of the paper; the random ranges are random_proximal_distal and random_proximal_proximal.
  • IZ (SNNModels 2.2.0). Default ge, gi start at zero; they were random with means 40 and 200 and every default IZ diverged.
  • HH (SNNModels 2.2.0). The 0/0 point of the rate functions (v exactly -50, -23 or -48 mV) gives its limit instead of NaN.
  • score_spikes (SNNUtils 1.0.1). The counting window follows window and delay; outputs differ from earlier ones only for delay > 10 ms, delay = nothing, or windows ending past offset_times[end] + 100.

Improved

  • Multipod (SNNModels 2.3.0). New Multipod (soma with 1 to 64 passive dendrites), MultipodParameter, MultipodPaper, declare_multipod, set_dendrite_length!, ndendrites; equal to Tripod for two dendrites, bit for bit. Re-exported as SNN.<name>.
  • vSTDP delay and triplet option (SNNModels 2.3.0). delay_uv of vSTDPParameter and vSTDPDenseParameter; nearest of STDPTriplet. Voltage rules on a Tripod, Multipod or BallAndStick compartment read that compartment.
  • TripodPaper (SNNModels 2.1.0). TripodPaper, PaperScheme (the integration scheme of the published code, field scheme of Tripod), HeunScheme, mouse synapses (DuarteGluDend, TripodDendSynapseMouse, MouseNMDA), NMDAVoltageDependency(approx), ReceptorSynapse(decay), dendrite diameter d, El_dend, 1000 um length guard. Re-exported as SNN.<name>.
  • Synapses and API (SNNModels 2.2.0). Field exact of ReceptorSynapse; SynapseNormalization about 7 times faster; firing_rate of a silent population returns zeros; positional AggregateScalingParameter accepts Wmax; record on an empty record and monitor! on a key already monitored give explicit messages; dendrite lengths of any Real type; thread-safe receptor registry.
  • SNNUtils 1.1.0. Submodule SNNUtils.SpikeTimit (Spike TIMIT reader, migrated from SpikeTimit.jl; remove that package from the environment). create_dataset stores Transcription objects, select_words samples without replacement by default, get_ordered_spikes takes seconds.
  • CalciumSpike 1.1.0. seed keyword of calcium_trace, generate_synthetic_data, biophysical_calcium; compat SNNModels = "1.7.2, 2".
  • Compat SNNModels = "2.3".

2.0.1 (SpikingNeuralNetworks 2.0.1; SNNModels 2.0.1 to 2.0.3; SNNUtils 1.0.1)

Fixed

  • STTC (SNNModels 2.0.1). Both trains are restricted to the interval and the total time is the union of the windows (Cutts and Eglen 2014); 2.0.0 returned values above 1 in absolute value and NaN.
  • Perturbation windows (SNNModels 2.0.2). Window numbers are never reused; after deleting window 2 of {1, 2, 3} the next flush overwrote window 3.

Improved

  • Perturbation records (SNNModels 2.0.2). One PerturbationStore / PerturbationSpikeStore per (object, variable, condition); perturbation_windows, compact_perturbation_records! (one-time conversion of older files). Output of perturbation_record unchanged.
  • :fire records (SNNModels 2.0.3). Neuron ids stored as Int32; monitor_time sizes the :fire buffer; get_git_commit_hash(; dir) and provenance of write_config from outside the repository.
  • monitor_rate (SNNModels 2.0.1) carries units (50Hz); a plain number is an ArgumentError.
  • Performance comparison page.

2.0.0 (SNNModels 2.0.0, SpikingNeuralNetworks 2.0.0, SNNUtils 1.0.0, SNNPlots 1.0.0, CalciumSpike 1.0.0)

Fixed

  • Random streams. Poisson stimuli and populations draw one random number per spike: same laws, different streams, so seeded 1.9 runs are not reproduced.
  • Delays. Delays are rounded up to whole steps (ceil(delay / dt)); at dt such as 0.1 ms, 1.9.1 delivered 22 to 80 % of the events one step late.
  • Plasticity. STDP weights differ from 1.9.1 by Float32 rounding (lazy traces); vSTDPParameter is event-driven (the 1.9.1 rule is vSTDPDenseParameter).
  • Tripod and BallAndStick use param.physiology.

Improved

  • Speed and memory. E/I balanced network 8 to 12 times faster than 1.9.1; synapse memory 29 to 9 bytes per synapse; event-driven vSTDP, ring-buffer delays, one spike list per population, vectorised Tripod and BallAndStick, flush-to-zero of subnormals, no allocation per step.
  • New. Float64 by population type; with_plasticity, without_plasticity, change_plasticity; opt-in threading (neurons_per_thread).
  • Removed. iSTDPTime; code that was not loaded (Multipod of 1.x, IF_CANAHP, AdExMultiTimescale, VariableInputParameter, stale SNNUtils parameter sets, SNNPlots Plots.jl backend).
  • Upgrading from 1.x. Files saved with version 1 are refused at load: rebuild the model from its configuration. Read plasticity state with plasticity_value, trace_values, sync_traces!. Call SNNModels.ensure_transpose!(syn) before syn.J / syn.index; read syn.ρ with SNNModels.efficacy. syn.param.delaytime has length 1 when all delays are equal. For Float64 build populations with VFT = Vector{Float64} and rules with FT = Float64. flush_subnormals = false restores IEEE behaviour. Positional constructors of the changed structs need the new fields. Do not write pop.fire of an integrate-and-fire population between its integrate! and the end of the step.

1.9.0 (SNNModels 1.9.0, SNNPlots 0.2.11, SNNUtils 0.2.10, SpikingNeuralNetworks 1.3.0)

Fixed

  • Tripod and BallAndStick. The somatic exponential term is gL ΔT exp((V - θ)/ΔT) (it lacked gL): rheobase -29 % (1134 to 814 pA), rate at 1500 pA 24 to 44 Hz, E rate of a 400-cell E/I network doubled. The Heun stages are consistent (spike times moved by up to 7 ms over 500 ms). Refractory counters keep at least one step per period. New: Vspike (default -10 mV then), dendritic El used, Is/Id of BallAndStick applied.
  • Tsodyks-Markram STP. MarkramSTPParameter* follow Mongillo et al. (2008): the same u^+ sets the efficacy u^+ x and the depletion of x; before, the efficacy used u before the jump (first spike from rest U(2 - U) instead of U; with U = 0.3, τF = 500 ms, τD = 70 ms a 20 Hz train facilitated before and depresses now).
  • Membrane parameters. C, gl, R, τm of AdExParameter and IFParameter are consistent (τm = C/gl, R = 1nS/gl): construction takes a pair, one value alone is an ArgumentError, more than two must agree within 0.1 %; changing one value updates the others (τm keeps gl, C keeps gl, gl or R keeps C). @update! cfg.adex.τm = x on a Tripod had no effect and sets C = τm gl now; AdExParameter(gl = 40nS, τm = 20ms) gives C = 800pF (was 281 pF). New: with_membrane, resolve_membrane, membrane_update.
  • HH and MorrisLecar flag one spike per action potential (about 60 at dt = 0.01 ms); Rate and WilsonCowan reset g every step; Confavreux2025Synapse increments by w (was w dt).
  • AggregateScaling time constants in ms, rate units, correct summed weight; HetRec baseline with dt; AdditiveNorm restores the summed weight.
  • vSTDP and iSTDPPotential voltage traces start at the membrane potential; time axis of record / interpolated_record exact; SNNPlots stdp_test / stdp_kernel measure one pair (the kernel included an extra causal pairing).
  • SNNUtils quaresima2022_nar returns Tripod keyword arguments.

Improved

  • BalancedStimulus, FORCE and PINning connections, train! with IZ/HH/MorrisLecar or without connections, connections onto MorrisLecar/ExtendedIF/WilsonCowan, several analysis, IO and SNNUtils functions work; 35 exported but undefined names were removed.

Earlier 1.x releases

Fixed

  • iSTDPRate (SNNModels 1.5.0 to 1.8.1). Potentiation at a postsynaptic spike was applied to the wrong synapses; rerun simulations that used it.
  • STDPGerstner (before SNNModels 1.8.2). Amplitudes were applied twice (effective A^2, sign lost); old amplitude A corresponds to ±A^2 now.

Improved

  • Notes of SpikingNeuralNetworks 1.2.1, SNNModels 1.8.2 to 1.8.4 and SNNPlots 0.2.10: release notes of SpikingNeuralNetworks 1.3.0.
